      SEG 07Questions

      Before you ask.

      Is this financial advice?

      No. BREAKING measures news coverage. It never tells you to buy anything, and a burn says nothing about where a price goes.

      Are the burns automatic?

      The orders are. They’re computed from public data every 15 minutes and come out the same for everyone. At launch the burn wallet fills them by hand from creator fees, and every fill shows up on the record with its transaction. A bot that fills orders on its own is next on the roadmap.

      Where does the data come from?

      The GDELT Project, a free and open index of the world’s online news that updates every 15 minutes. We use its share-of-coverage timelines, so a spike means a word is taking a bigger slice of all news, not just that more news got written that day.

      Can someone fake a spike?

      Not cheaply. A spike needs a measurable share of the world’s coverage to move at once. A blog post or a wave of posts on X doesn’t register.

      Why these twelve words?

      They’re names, markets and tech that spike often and for many reasons. We left out wars, attacks and disasters on purpose. Nobody should profit from a tragedy trending.

      What if the creator fees run out?

      Orders wait in line and fill oldest first when fees come in. A daily cap, shown on the record, keeps one wild news day from draining the desk.

      Will BREAKING DM me?

      Never. There’s no presale, no airdrop and no support account. Anyone messaging you as BREAKING is not us.
